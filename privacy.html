RVAULT Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

RVAULT is a student-developed application for collecting and organizing content shared through supported social-media messaging integrations.

Information We Receive

When an Instagram account is connected and messaging access is enabled, RVAULT may receive webhook data provided by Instagram, such as sender and recipient identifiers, message timestamps, message text, and shared content information.

How We Use Information

RVAULT uses received information to provide its content-saving functionality, including identifying shared URLs, preventing duplicate saves, and organizing saved content.

Storage

During development, saved content is stored in the application's database. Information is not intentionally sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

RVAULT may use Meta's Instagram APIs and webhooks to receive supported Instagram messaging events. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved content is retained until it is deleted by the application owner or the application's storage is cleared. For privacy or deletion requests, please contact the RVAULT project owner through the project's GitHub repository.

Security

RVAULT is developed with reasonable safeguards for a student project. Access tokens and other secrets are intended to be stored outside source control and must not be committed to the public repository.

Children's Privacy

RVAULT is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as RVAULT's functionality changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact options available through the RVAULT project's GitHub repository.
